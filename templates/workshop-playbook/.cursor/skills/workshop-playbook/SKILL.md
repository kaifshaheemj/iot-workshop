Do not invent a new UI. Keep `styles.css`, `index.html`, and `app.js` unchanged. Rewrite `content.js` from `CONTEXT.md` only.

For the one-day university session in the parent repo, fill **one** file in `workshop/contexts/` and follow `PROMPT.md`. Do not spoil afternoon PoC names on morning pages.
