Reaction Arena sketches

1. Open 01_led_chase/01_led_chase.ino and prove LEDs 1-4 on GPIO 16, 17, 18 and 19.
2. Open 02_button_test/02_button_test.ino and prove the matching buttons on GPIO 25, 26, 27 and 32.
3. Open reaction.ino for the complete 1-4 player, three-round Wi-Fi arena.

Serial Monitor uses 115200 baud. Join the open Wi-Fi network "Reaction Arena" and open the IP printed by Serial. The final build intentionally does not use an OLED.
